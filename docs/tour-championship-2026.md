# Drop No. 01 — 2026 TOUR Championship research file

Compiled 2 Aug 2026. Full sourcing in links; facts verified against two or more
independent sources.

## The event

- **Competition rounds: Thu 27 – Sun 30 August 2026** (gates Wed 26). One week
  later than 2025. [PGA Tour](https://www.pgatour.com/tournaments/2026/tour-championship/R2026060/overview) ·
  [tourchampionship.com](https://www.tourchampionship.com/news/2026/04/16/tickets-on-sale-2026-tour-championship-pga-tour-east-lake-atlanta)
- East Lake Golf Club, Atlanta. FedEx Cup final 30, straight stroke play (no
  starting strokes), $40M purse / $10M to the winner.
- Defending: **Tommy Fleetwood**, −18 in 2025 — his first PGA Tour win, on his 164th start.
- Trophy: sterling replica of **Calamity Jane**, Bobby Jones's hickory-shafted
  putter — the one club that survived the 1925 clubhouse fire.
- **Scarcity angle (verified June 2026):** the finale moves to rotating venues
  from 2028; East Lake is contracted through 2027. 2026 is one of the last two
  annual East Lake editions. Don't overstate — East Lake may return in rotation.

## Trademark guardrails

- Never name a garment or collection **"Bobby Jones"** — name/likeness licensed
  by Jonesheirs, Inc. to an existing apparel brand. Editorial mention is fine.
- "TOUR Championship", "FedExCup", "East Lake Golf Club" — reference
  descriptively; no logo-alike lockups; avoid FedEx purple/orange entirely.

## Iconography worth drawing

Calamity Jane's silhouette (hickory shaft, twine whipping, small blade), the
Shutze Tudor gable, Ross grass-faced deep-well bunkers, the 1930 Grand Slam
date-mark, 1920s pennant typography.
